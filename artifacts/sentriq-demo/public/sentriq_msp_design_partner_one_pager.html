Sentriq · MSP design partner brief

Make smaller clients profitable to serve.

Sentriq is an AI security-operations layer that helps MSPs extend reliable protection into the 6–20 employee market—without adding analysts in proportion to every new endpoint.

More endpoints. Fewer analysts. Automation underneath your service. Your team stays in control.

The problem Sentriq is built around

Small accounts are costly

Onboarding, ticketing, monitoring, and reporting consume too much human time for the revenue a small account generates.

Alert volume does not scale

More endpoints create more low-value triage, pulling technicians away from escalations and client relationships.

The market is moving down

MSPs need new customers and smaller deal sizes are becoming more common, but traditional delivery economics have not caught up.

What Sentriq does

It works with the security tools already deployed at a client, then turns scattered signals into a prioritized, explainable operating queue.

01 · DEPLOYDiscover endpoints and push standard configurations.
02 · DETECTPull signals from CrowdStrike, Defender, and other EDR tools.
03 · INVESTIGATECorrelate events and produce evidence-backed rationale.
04 · RESPONDAutomate routine remediation; route high-impact actions for approval.
05 · REPORTGenerate clear client-facing status and activity reporting.

Why an MSP would care

  • Increase endpoints per technician
  • Reduce manual alert-triage time
  • Onboard accounts with repeatable configs
  • Make sub-20-employee clients viable

What Sentriq does not do

  • Replace your EDR or detection vendor
  • Take over the client relationship
  • Hide decisions behind a black box
  • Contain or isolate systems without approval

Proposed design-partner pilot

Co-design the safe operating model on live work.

Start with one primary EDR integration and a representative client set. Your technicians review the agent’s rationale, tune confidence thresholds, and identify where automation is genuinely safe. Sentriq remains underneath your service model; you retain the customer, policy, and final say.
